Offline Document Scanner Privacy Policy

Effective: July 21, 2026

Introduction

This application provides on-device document scanning, text recognition (OCR), and PDF export.

Camera

The app uses the device camera, with the user's permission, to capture images of physical documents for scanning.

On-Device Processing

Document scanning, OCR, and PDF generation are performed entirely on-device using Google ML Kit and local processing. Scanned images, recognized text, and generated PDFs are not transmitted to the developer's servers.

Local Storage

Scanned documents and related data are stored locally on the device in an encrypted database. If app lock is enabled, the PIN is stored as a salted hash and is never transmitted or recoverable by the developer.

Sharing & Export

Users may choose to share or print exported PDFs/images using their device's native share and print functionality. This is initiated by the user and is not data collection by the developer.

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases via Google Play Billing to unlock additional features. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play; the developer does not receive payment card details.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal information, scanned content, or usage data. The app does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.

Advertising & Analytics

The app does not display advertisements and does not use analytics or tracking technologies.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: contact@butterssoft.com.
