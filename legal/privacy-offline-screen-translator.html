Offline Screen Translator Privacy Policy

Effective: July 16, 2026

Introduction

This application provides on-device text recognition and translation of content displayed on the user's screen.

Screen Capture

The app uses Android screen capture functionality only after the user grants permission. Captured screen content is processed locally on the device to identify and translate text.

On-Device Processing

Translation and text recognition are performed using Google ML Kit technologies and locally stored language resources. Screen captures, recognized text, and translation results are not transmitted to the developer's servers.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information, screenshots, recognized text, translation results, or usage data.

Display Over Other Apps Permission

The "Display over other apps" permission is used solely to display translation results over other applications.

Advertising & Analytics

The app does not display advertisements and does not use analytics or tracking technologies.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: contact@butterssoft.com.
