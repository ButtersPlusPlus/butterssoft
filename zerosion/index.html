About the Game

This Tetris-like block puzzle game is the only one that adds up. Adds up to zero.

Drop numbered pieces onto the board and clear lines and squares by making their sums equal zero, not just by filling them. Positive and negative blocks, power-ups, and daily challenges keep every board a different puzzle.

What Makes It Different

Clearing means summing.

Lines and squares don't clear just because they're full — the numbers on the pieces have to add up to zero first.

Positive and negative blocks.

Every piece pushes the sum up or down, so placement is a balancing act as much as a fit.

Power-ups and daily challenges.

New tools and a fresh challenge board every day keep the puzzle from ever feeling solved.

About butterssoft

Formed as a solo indie game developer in Den Haag, Netherlands, butterssoft makes games with unique mechanics or takes an established genre and puts an interesting twist on it. Conspintrix is the first release with a few more puzzle based games soon to come, and after that some parody games that aim to make players laugh.
