About the Game

Rock paper scissors was never meant to go this deep. Necroshambo drags roshambo out of the grave and deals it back as a turn-based card battler with rot in its veins.

Your hand is a fistful of rock, paper, and scissors cards...but nothing stays fresh forever. Lose a throw and your card decays: fresh, then decaying, then rotted, then gone. Win with your staler card and your opponent pays double. Every round is one simple question with ugly consequences: which card can you afford to lose?

That one rule turns rock paper scissors into a real strategy card game: bluffing, sacrifice, and hand management built on the one ruleset everybody on earth already knows. Easy to learn. Surprisingly hard to master.

Choose your battlefield

  • Fight the Dead: Solo card battles against an AI that adapts to your habits. It learns what you throw. Stop throwing it.
  • Duel Your Friends: Multiplayer matches with a simple invite code. Send it to a friend, ruin the friendship.
  • Climb the Ladder: Ranked matchmaking with a real rating on the line. Face opponents at your level and take what's theirs.
  • Play at Your Own Pace: Async matches with a full day per move. Make your play, get on with your life, and get a nudge when it's your turn. A PvP card game that fits around your schedule, not the other way round. Quicker timed matchmaking in the works!
