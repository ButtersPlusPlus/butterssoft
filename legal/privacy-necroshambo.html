Necroshambo

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 23, 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy applies to the Necroshambo app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by butterssoft (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a service with optional in-app purchases. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device. The Application does not display advertisements and does not collect an advertising identifier.

To enable online multiplayer matches, the Application uses Supabase Authentication to assign your device an anonymous account. This account is not linked to any personal information such as your name or email address, and exists solely to identify you within a match and to store your gameplay record.

When you play an online match, the Application stores a display name (randomly generated at first, and changeable by you in the app), your win/loss record, your matchmaking rating, your badges, the moves and results of your matches, and any cosmetic items you've unlocked or equipped, associated with your anonymous account. This information is stored using Supabase. Your display name, rating, and equipped cosmetics are visible to opponents you are matched against; the contents of your hand during a match are never visible to your opponent until the match ends.

Once each time you open the Application, it records that your anonymous account was active that day, so that daily-play badges and streaks can be awarded. This is stored with your anonymous account and is used only for that purpose.

If you report or block another player, the Application stores that report or block (including a snapshot of the reported display name) with your anonymous account, so that the Service Provider can review reports and so that blocked players are not matched with you again. Display names are checked against a list of banned words.

The Application also stores game settings, local match history, and owned cosmetic items locally on your device. This local data does not leave your device except as described above. It is stored in an unencrypted local database that holds only preferences and statistics.

Push Notifications

On Android and iOS devices, the Application may register your device for push notifications — for example, to let you know it's your turn in an ongoing match — using Firebase Cloud Messaging (and, on iOS, the Apple Push Notification service). This requires storing a device push token associated with your anonymous account. You can decline this at the operating system's permission prompt; the Application functions fully without it, including in-app awareness of your ongoing matches.

Purchases

The Application offers optional in-app purchases of cosmetic items, such as alternate hand styles, card backs, and table backdrops. All payments are processed by the platform's app store — Google Play Billing on Android, or Apple's App Store / StoreKit on iOS — under that store's own privacy policy; the Service Provider does not collect or store your payment card details. The Application validates your purchases against the relevant app store's servers and records the resulting entitlement against your anonymous account, so that purchased items can be granted to you and, if you reinstall the Application or switch devices, restored. A local record of your unlocked and currently equipped items is also stored on your device.

Third Party Access

The Application utilizes third-party services that have their own privacy policies about handling data. Below are links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the Application:

  • Google Play Services (Android)
  • Supabase (multiplayer accounts, match data, cosmetic ownership)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store (for in-app purchases)

The Service Provider may disclose information collected by the Application:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • When the Service Provider believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • With trusted service providers who work on the Service Provider's behalf, have no independent use of the information disclosed to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set out in this privacy policy.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it, using the standard uninstall process for your device or app marketplace. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data immediately. Your anonymous online account and its associated match history, rating, and cosmetic ownership remain on the Service Provider's servers until you delete your account — see Data Retention Policy below.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain your online account data (display name, match record, rating, badges, cosmetic ownership, push token, and any reports or blocks you have made) for as long as you use the Application.

You can delete your account yourself at any time in the Application under Settings → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your anonymous account and the data associated with it from the Service Provider's servers, including your display name, rating, badges, cosmetic ownership, purchase records, push token, and your reports and blocks; any match you are currently playing is forfeited first. Completed matches you only joined as a guest keep their result but are no longer linked to you. Deleting your account does not refund purchases, but because purchases belong to your store account, you can restore them to a new account using Restore in the in-app store. Deleting the Application alone does not delete your online account. If you cannot use the in-app option, you can instead request deletion by contacting contact@butterssoft.com, and the Service Provider will respond in a reasonable time. Apple and Google retain their own purchase records under their own policies.

Children

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the event the Service Provider discovers that a child under 13 has provided personal information, the Service Provider will immediately delete this from their servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider at contact@butterssoft.com so they can take the necessary action.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information, and provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information it processes and maintains. Data exchanged between the Application and its servers is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you are consenting to the processing of your information as set forth in this Privacy Policy now and as amended by the Service Provider. You may delete your account and its data at any time in the Application (Settings → Delete Account), or request access to or deletion of your data by contacting contact@butterssoft.com.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy while using the Application, or questions about these practices, please contact the Service Provider by email at contact@butterssoft.com.
