About the Game

CubiClear is butterssoft's next puzzle game, currently in development for Android and iOS. Details on the core mechanic are still under wraps, but the shape of the game is set: work through a structured Campaign of levels, unwind in an open-ended Free Play mode, or take on a once-a-day Daily Challenge and see how your time stacks up against everyone else's.

Ways to play

  • Campaign: A structured sequence of levels that tracks your completion, best times, and mistake counts as you work through it.
  • Free Play: Jump in and solve puzzles at your own pace, no level structure required.
  • Daily Challenge: One puzzle a day, with your completion time ranked anonymously on a shared leaderboard.

Features

  • Campaign progress, best times, and mistake tracking, saved locally on your device
  • Daily Challenge with an anonymous, session-based global leaderboard
  • A global "lives" system that gates retries after a mistake, with an optional one-time purchase to remove it
  • Rewarded ads to refill lives or unlock a hint, entirely optional
  • Free to play, ad-supported, with the same privacy-first ad consent controls as Zerosion

What Makes It Different

Three ways to play.

A structured Campaign, a no-pressure Free Play mode, and a competitive Daily Challenge, all built on the same core puzzle.

Fair, not punishing.

Lives gate retries, but a rewarded ad or a single optional purchase is all it takes to play without limits.

Privacy-first, as always.

The same ad consent controls, anonymous leaderboard identity, and on-device data storage as every other butterssoft game.

About butterssoft

Formed as a solo indie game developer in Den Haag, Netherlands, butterssoft makes games with unique mechanics or takes an established genre and puts an interesting twist on it. Conspintrix and Zerosion are the first releases, with CubiClear and a few more puzzle-based games soon to come, and after that some parody games that aim to make players laugh.
